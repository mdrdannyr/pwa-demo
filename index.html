    Account & Drive

    Sign in once to back up your state in your own Google Drive. This also grants the app access to that one state file — nothing else in your Drive.

    Local only

    Sharing with others

    The state file stays in your Drive. You can invite people as editors, and their changes merge with yours.

    1. Invite a collaborator by email below. Google sends them a notification with access to PWA Demo state.json.
    2. They open this app, come to this Setup page, and click Open shared file. Google's narrow Drive permission requires this one-time step.
    3. In the picker they choose Shared with me and select that JSON file. After that, edits sync automatically; the app checks for updates every 60 seconds.

    You can also manage or revoke access at any time in Google Drive.

    Tasks are saved on this device and synced to your Google Drive when connected.
